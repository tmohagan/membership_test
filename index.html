Understanding Membership Tiers

THE CLUB CARD
$55/year
  • Everyday low prices
  • Curbside Pickup (Free w/ $50+)
  • NO Rewards Earning
  • NO Gas Discount (Standard)
BEST VALUE
THE CLUB+ CARD
$110/year
  • 2% Back in Rewards (on most purchases)
  • 5¢ off/gal at BJ's Gas
  • 2 Free Same-Day Deliveries/Year ($20 value)
  • Events where rewards double
Upsell Logic: If a member spends $150/week, they earn ~$156/year in rewards. The upgrade pays for itself!
